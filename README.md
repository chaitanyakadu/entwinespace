## Introduction
**Entwinespace** is an **open-source 2D virtual workspace software**, designed to make collaboration seamless. To get started, simply create and customize the server(virtual community), invite people and start collaborating.

---
## Details
> **Entwinespace is build to increase the productivity via seamless collaboration.**

The software utilizes 2D metaverse for visualization and the end-to-end encrypted VoIP for real-time and secure communication. The 2D metaverse features a minimal design with elements such as characters, rooms, and workspace. The platform allows you to communicate via audio calls, video calls and instant messaging. It also offers a range of customization options, allowing you to control who can see and hear you, as well as how they do so. 
The platform also has a bunch of feature's which further amplify the productivity, where ai-peer and plugins are the primary ones. Ai-peer is basically an AI agent which works as a peer/employee with a more human like predictable behavior. We also have plugins like the github and spotify.

## Key features
- Completely open-source

- Multiple plugins (github, notion, spotify..)

## Story
> _The Latin word "Collaboration" formed by the combination of "com (together) + laborare (to work)"  literally means "to work together"._

In my perspective, collaboration has been the foundation of human survival and evolution. From hunting large animals in groups for survival to achieving the desired power and scale, we have come a long way collaborating.

In this era of online collaboration, it has become incredibly easy to collaborate but when it comes to achieving the goals and making an impact we witness some kind of friction. A friction caused due to lack of structured and seamless interaction.

This is where **EntwineSpace** comes forward and pitchs itself as a tool which provides you a structured and seamless interaction.
